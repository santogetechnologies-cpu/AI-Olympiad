Page 4 – So, What Is AI?
🖼️ Image idea: A computer with a glowing brain inside, connected by lines to a phone, a
car and a robot.
AI stands for Artificial Intelligence.
Artificial = made by people, not born in nature
Intelligence = the ability to learn and solve problems
So AI is a computer or machine that can do smart things, like recognising pictures,
understanding speech or making decisions.
💡 Did You Know? The name "Artificial Intelligence" was first used by scientists in 1956, a
long time before you were born!
⭐ Remember: AI = machines that can learn and act smart.

Page 6 – Data: The Food of AI 🍎
🖼️ Image idea: A hungry robot eating pictures, numbers and words from a big plate.
You need food to grow. AI needs data to learn.
Data means information such as photos, numbers, words, sounds and videos.
More good examples → smarter AI
Wrong or unfair examples → confused AI
💡 Did You Know? To learn what a cat looks like, an AI may look at thousands of cat
photos!
✏️ Try It! List 3 kinds of data: one you can see, one you can hear, one you can count.
Page 7 – How Does a Machine Learn? 🐱🐶
🖼️ Image idea: Three steps drawn as cartoon panels: photos of cats and dogs → robot
studying → robot correctly saying "Cat!"
Let's teach a computer to tell cats from dogs:
1. Show examples – photos labelled "cat" or "dog"
2. Practise – the computer finds patterns (pointy ears, whiskers, snouts)
3. Test – we show a new photo and it guesses
4. Improve – if it is wrong, it learns and tries again
This way of learning from examples is called Machine Learning.
🧠 Think Fast! How is this like you learning to recognise fruits?

Page 9 – Oops! AI Can Make Mistakes ⚠️
🖼️ Image idea: A robot confused, holding a muffin while a picture of a puppy is beside it
with a question mark.
AI is clever, but it is not perfect.
It can guess wrong if it did not see enough examples
It can be unfair if its data was unfair
It does not truly "understand" like a person
That is why humans must always check what AI says and decide wisely.

muffins because they look a bit alike!

Page 20 – Sense, Think, Act! 👀🧠💪
🖼️ Image idea: A robot with arrows pointing to its eyes (camera), brain (computer) and
arms (motors).
Smart machines work in 3 steps:
1. SENSE 👀 – cameras, microphones and sensors collect information
2. THINK 🧠 – the computer (with AI) decides what to do
3. ACT 💪 – motors, wheels or speakers carry out the action
Example – Robot vacuum: Sense: finds a wall → Think: "turn left" → Act: turns and keeps
cleaning.
✏️ Try It! Write the Sense-Think-Act steps for a traffic light that detects cars.

Page 24 – Using AI Safely and Kindly 🛡️💛
🖼️ Image idea: A child and a robot shaking hands under a shield with a heart on it.
Golden rules for smart kids:
1. 🔒 Never share private information (address, passwords, school name) with AI.
2. 👨‍👩‍👧 Ask an adult before using new apps.
3. ✅ Check facts. AI can be wrong.
4. 🧠 Do your own thinking. Use AI to help, not to copy.
5. 💛 Be kind. Use polite words, and never use AI to hurt anyone.
