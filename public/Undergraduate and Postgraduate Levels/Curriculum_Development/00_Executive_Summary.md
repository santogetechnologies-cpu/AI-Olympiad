# AI Olympiad Higher Education Curriculum — Master Executive Index
## Complete Curriculum for Undergraduate (UG 1–4) & Postgraduate (PG 1–2) Levels

---

### Executive Overview
This master repository contains the comprehensive, professionally developed **AI Olympiad Higher Education Curriculum** covering all six university academic years:
- **Undergraduate Tier:** UG 1st Year, UG 2nd Year, UG 3rd Year, UG Final Year (48 lessons)
- **Postgraduate Tier:** PG 1st Year, PG Final Year (24 lessons)
- **Total Learning Modules:** 72 lessons across 36 chapter modules, authored with full mathematical rigor, complete runnable code harnesses, interactive gamified missions, and authentic Olympiad assessments.

Every lesson strictly implements the mandatory **14-point curriculum specification**, translating the interactive mission-driven learning philosophy of the Class 3 reference benchmark into university-grade simulations, tournaments, and research challenges.
