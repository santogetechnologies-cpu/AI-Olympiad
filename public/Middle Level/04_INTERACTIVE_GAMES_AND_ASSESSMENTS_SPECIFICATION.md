# Middle Level AI Olympiad: Interactive Games & Assessment Specification
## Classes 6, 7, and 8 — Complete 36-Lesson Gamification & Evaluation Blueprint

---
### 1. Executive Pedagogical Architecture & Framework Overview
The Middle Level AI Olympiad learning experience elevates student engagement from passive reading into active, hands-on inquiry. By combining **original, topic-specific game engines** with **rigorous Olympiad-standard assessments**, students develop both intuitive problem-solving instincts and technical mastery.

#### 1.1 The Tri-Level Progression Dynamic
- **Class 6 (Foundational Reasoning & Guided Exploration):** Emphasizes rule-vs-data gauntlets, visual mazes, simulated sensor telemetry, and digital privacy shields. Mechanics prioritize visual clarity and structured choices.
- **Class 7 (Algorithmic Logic, Data Forensics & Prompt Engineering):** Introduces matrix arithmetic games, financial fraud forensics, energy grid balance dispatchers, syntax escape rooms, prompt arena tournaments, and demographic fairness audits.
- **Class 8 (Decision Boundaries, System Architecture & Digital Rights):** Focuses on mathematical hyperplane boundary builders, diagnostic clinic triage, modular microservices, neural media studios, deepfake forensic courtrooms, and data sovereignty enforcement terminals.

#### 1.2 Cognitive Domain Assessment Matrix (Bloom's Taxonomy for AI Olympiad)
| Cognitive Level | Weightage (Class 6) | Weightage (Class 7) | Weightage (Class 8) | Focus & Question Anatomy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L1: Knowledge & Conceptual Recall** | 30% | 20% | 15% | Formal definitions, architecture landmarks, core terminology. |
| **L2: Algorithmic & Logical Reasoning** | 30% | 30% | 25% | Step-by-step logic tracing, decision-tree branch evaluation. |
| **L3: Code Trace & Execution Analysis** | 15% | 25% | 30% | Dry-running Python snippets, debugging syntax/logic bugs. |
| **L4: System Design & Edge-Case Evaluation** | 15% | 15% | 20% | Metric trade-offs (precision/recall, bias, overfitting). |
| **L5: Ethical, Legal & Responsible AI Audit** | 10% | 10% | 10% | Privacy laws (GDPR/DPDP), dark patterns, deepfake detection. |
