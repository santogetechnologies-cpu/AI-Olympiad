# AI Olympiad Middle Level (Classes 6–8)
## Deliverable 2: Detailed Syllabus Mapping & Curriculum Framework

---

### Framework Overview

The Middle Level AI Olympiad curriculum is engineered for students in Classes 6, 7, and 8 (ages 11–14). The framework follows a six-domain spiral architecture that deepens across the three years:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        AI OLYMPIAD 6-DOMAIN SPIRAL                     │
├────────────────────┬────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. AI Basics       │ 2. Communicate AI  │ 3. AI Applications           │
│ (Foundations)      │ (Programming/NLP)  │ (Real-World Domains)         │
├────────────────────┼────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. AI and Career   │ 5. AI Tools        │ 6. Responsible AI            │
│ (Future Pathways)  │ (Hands-on Systems) │ (Ethics, Bias, Governance)   │
└────────────────────┴────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```
